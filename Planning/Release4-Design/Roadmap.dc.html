Release goals and exit criteria

Each release ends when a test a buyer would run passes.

R4 · Weeks 1–6

Storefront & shell

A prospect learns what WorkOrder does and costs before signing up, and never meets test data.

Done when
  • Pricing is one click from the landing page
  • Production market shows zero QA fixtures
  • Field keeps the console shell at every width
R5 · Weeks 7–12

Pilot-ready

A design-partner company onboards its crew without us on the phone.

Done when
  • Accounts verify email and recover passwords
  • New users set a rate and one skill before bidding
  • Offers, awards and releases reach email
R6 · Weeks 13–20

Paid pilot

[N] pilot organizations pay on the plan chosen in D1.

Done when
  • Plan limits are enforced on the server
  • Subscription and scope fees reconcile with Stripe
  • Org owners can export their data
R7 · Weeks 21–26

General availability

Anyone can sign up, pay and get help without us in the loop.

Done when
  • Core flows pass a WCAG 2.2 AA audit
  • A restore from backup completes on schedule
  • Upgrade to a paid plan works end to end
