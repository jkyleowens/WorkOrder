How it works

From posted job to released payment, one scope at a time.

01

Split the job

Post a project and break it into nested scopes. Scopes stay editable until the first bid arrives.

02

Bid per scope

Solo contractors and organizations bid on the scopes that fit their trade. The client awards each one.

03

Work in the field

Crews clock in against a scope from a phone. Entries queue with no signal and sync when it returns.

04

Fund and release

Clients fund a scope through Stripe and release payment against approved pay applications, lien waivers attached.
