02 · Product thesis

WorkOrder is the job ledger for trade work: every project is a tree of scopes, and every scope carries its own bid, crew, hours, documents and money.

Pays

Trade organizations

Need job costing, crew time and compliant billing without juggling three tools. They buy seats.

Supply

Independent contractors

Need work that fits their trade and proof they will be paid. Free to join, visible once profiled.

Demand

Clients posting work

Need to split a job, fund it in stages and see progress. Fees apply when a scope is funded.

Positioning line from the review: WorkOrder replaces the spreadsheet, the group text and the separate invoicing tool.

03 · Design principles for this iteration

Finish the surface area that exists.

1

One shell, every context

Field, office and client views share the sidebar, header and breadcrumb. A full-screen field mode is something a crew chooses, never the side effect of a nav click.

2

The scope tree is the headline

Nested scopes lead the marketing page, open the project page and get a one-time explainer. The most defensible thing we built should be the most visible.

3

Persuade outside, label inside

Marketing voice lives before login. After it, headings are plain nouns an operator scans on their twentieth visit: Billing, Time, Projects.

4

Show nothing until there is something

One setup checklist replaces per-section empty panels. Heavy components such as the 24-hour grid wait for their first record. Never fake data to fill space.

Built on the Industry system
Barlow and Barlow Condensed · square panels, no elevation · 3px controls · status is always text plus color
